# DR-022: Record `area`'s measured post-layout footprint and its `not met` disposition

- **Status**: proposed
- **Date**: 2026-08-11
- **Decided by**: Loom Builder agent, issue #211

## Context

[`spec/target-spec.md#area`](../target-spec.md#area) reads "Total block area
(both sub-blocks, excluding pads)" — **[TBD-#17]**, wave-1 planning budget
**≤0.05 mm² (≤50 000 µm²) [P]** — with its own prose stating the budget is "a
planning bound to be **replaced** by the measured post-layout number, not a
substitute for it." #17 closed the layout (drawn, assembled, DRC-clean and
LVS-matched against its schematic-derived reference, one device excluded per
#177), and #18's post-layout re-run of the whole verification suite has
completed, but the one measurement the row was always waiting on was never
taken or recorded anywhere in the repository. #211 is that measurement.

`klt stats` (klt 0.2.0) reports a cell's bounding-box footprint, total drawn
(union-of-layers) polygon area, density and polygon/vertex counts from the
committed GDS alone — no PDK deck needed, since it reads geometry, not device
recognition. #211 wires it into `layout/run_checks.sh` as a new step (run for
every cell alongside DRC/extraction/LVS) and commits the result as
`layout/reports/<cell>/stats.json`, gated by the same
`provenance.gds_sha256`-drift check (`lvs_reference.GDS_HASH_FIELDS`) that
already covers `drc.json`/`extract.json` — so the number this record ratifies
cannot silently drift from the GDS the way a hand-typed figure would.

Against the committed `layout/cells/temp_por_top.gds`:

```
top_cell: temp_por_top
bbox_um: (-140.0, -540.0) - (1194.0, 254.0)  1334.0 x 794.0
area_um2: 369261.93960000004
density: 0.3486247489605323
```

**The row's own wording is ambiguous among three measured numbers that differ
by up to ~3×**, so which one it means has to be stated, not left implicit:

| Measure | Value | vs. the ≤50 000 µm² planning bound |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `temp_por_top`'s own bounding-box footprint | **1 059 196 µm² = 1.059196 mm²** | **21.2×** |
| Sum of the four sub-cells' own bounding boxes (`bias_core` 124 250.93, `por_comparator` 73 291.50, `por_output_chain` 23 859.27, `temp_core` 120 313.83 µm²) | 341 715.53 µm² ≈ 0.341716 mm² | 6.8× |
| `klt stats`' drawn-polygon area (union of layers over the whole bbox — not a footprint) | 369 261.94 µm² ≈ 0.369262 mm² | 7.4× |

Per-cell footprints, from each cell's own `layout/reports/<cell>/stats.json`:
`bias_core` 434.9 × 285.7 µm, `por_comparator` 445.0 × 164.7 µm,
`por_output_chain` 225.3 × 105.9 µm, `temp_core` 569.25 × 211.355 µm. The
assembled footprint is ~3.1× the sum of the four sub-cells' own boxes, which
is consistent with `TOP_PLACEMENT` in `layout/build_cells.py` (`temp_core` at
`(0, 0)`, the POR row at `y = −400` spanning `x = 0…1145`) plus the full-width
`VDD`/`VSS` rails, the perimeter guard ring and the domain-seam moat — i.e.
the gap between the two readings is floorplan spacing, not a stray shape
inflating the box (see `layout/README.md` § `temp_por_top`, "The floorplan
re-derivation itself").

Under either of the two footprint-shaped readings the ≤0.05 mm² bound is
missed by a wide margin (21.2× or 6.8×); the conclusion "not met" does not
depend on resolving which convention the row means. The number recorded
against the row does.

## Decision

**`spec/target-spec.md#area`'s Target column adopts the assembled top-level
cell's own bounding-box footprint as this row's measurement convention**:
**1.059 mm² (1 059 196 µm²)**, measured from `temp_por_top.gds`
(`layout/reports/temp_por_top/stats.json`, regenerated by
`layout/run_checks.sh`). The `[TBD-#17]` placeholder is replaced by this
measured value. **The ≤0.05 mm² wave-1 planning budget is retained in the
row's text, unchanged, and recorded as not met (21.2× over)** — it is not
deleted and not silently relaxed. The row's status moves from `pending #17`
to `pending #1` (measured; #17's own job — producing the number — is done;
the row now awaits #1's overall ratification pass, the same convention every
other measured-but-unmet row in this table already uses, e.g.
[`temp-accuracy-untrimmed`](../target-spec.md#temp-accuracy-untrimmed)).

**Why the footprint, not the tiled sub-cell sum or the drawn-polygon area.**
"Total block area ... excluding pads" most naturally reads as *what the
delivered part occupies* — the number a shuttle-seat allocation or a
reticle floorplan would actually have to reserve, "excluding pads" scoping
out a future I/O ring this wave-1 design does not draw. That is the
assembled top cell's own bounding box, not an unassembled sum of its four
instances' boxes (which excludes the rails, guard ring and inter-instance
spacing the real, deliverable block also occupies) and not the drawn-polygon
area (which is smaller than even the sub-cell sum here, despite describing
the *larger*, assembled geometry, because most of the bbox between and
around the instances is unfilled space — reporting it as "the area" would
be actively misleading about what the block costs to place).

## Alternatives considered

- **Adopt the sum of the four sub-cells' own bounding boxes (0.342 mm²) as
  the row's convention.** Rejected. It undercounts real, unavoidable
  floorplan cost this design already pays — the full-width `VDD`/`VSS`
  rails, the perimeter guard ring, and the domain-seam moat isolating the
  temperature-sensing and POR domains (`layout/floorplan.md`'s own isolation
  plan) — so it does not answer "what does this block cost to place," which
  is the question the row exists to answer. It remains recorded in the table
  above as a real, useful cross-check number, just not the ratified
  convention.
- **Adopt `klt stats`' drawn-polygon (union-of-layers) area (0.369 mm²).**
  Rejected outright — `klt stats --help` itself distinguishes "bounding box"
  from "drawn area", and the latter is not a footprint at all. It happens to
  read *larger* than the sub-cell-sum alternative here only because
  `temp_por_top`'s own guard ring and rails add real drawn polygons the
  individual sub-cells' own footprints did not carry standalone; a metal
  layer's fill fraction moving would move this number with no floorplan
  change behind it, which is not a property "the area of the block" should
  have.
- **Re-cost the ≤0.05 mm² planning bound to a new ratified ceiling now,** the
  way [DR-018](DR-018-por-iq-recost.md) re-costed `por-iq` and
  [DR-019](DR-019-brownout-falling-slew-postlayout-recost.md) re-costed
  `por-brownout`'s falling-slew clause. Rejected for this row specifically.
  Both of those records moved a ceiling to a value carrying deliberate
  margin *just above* an already-close measured result (20.5% margin in each
  case) — the miss in both cases was a design detail away from the original
  intent. Here the measured footprint is **21.2× the planning bound**, not a
  close miss: there is no defensible "margin above measured" number to
  propose that both respects the original wave-1 planning rationale and
  covers the built part, and inventing a ~21×-larger bound with no design
  work behind the change is exactly the "unjustified inflation" DR-018's own
  Alternatives section rejected for its row. A future area target, if one is
  wanted, needs a resizing or floorplan decision behind it (a design
  question), not a spec-only re-cost.
- **Declare the row `ratifiable` as-is, treating "some measured area exists
  now" as sufficient.** Rejected. `pending #n` in this table's own legend
  means "evidence showing the row is not met", which is exactly this row's
  state — the planning bound is real, was never met, and marking it
  otherwise would misstate the evidence.
- **Delete the ≤0.05 mm² planning bound since it is now known to be
  unreachable at this design's current size.** Rejected, per the issue's own
  explicit instruction and CLAUDE.md ("agents do not relax the ratified spec
  to make results pass"): the bound is retained, unchanged, marked not met.
  Only an operator ruling can retire or replace it.
- **Leave the row `[TBD-#17]`/`pending #17` indefinitely**, on the reasoning
  that #17 already closed without filling it. Rejected — the measurement
  this record makes is one command against already-committed geometry, #17's
  closure is exactly what made it possible, and deferring further adds no
  information (the same reasoning DR-018's own Alternatives section gives
  for declining to leave `por-iq` `pending #1` indefinitely).

## Consequences

**No circuit or layout change results from this record.** This is a decision
about what to record against an already-built, already-measured layout, not
a commitment to shrink it. `temp_por_top.gds` is unchanged; `layout/reports/`
gains one new report type (`stats.json`) per cell, generated the same run as
the DRC/extraction/LVS reports it sits alongside.

**`area` moves from an unmeasured provisional budget to a measured,
ratified-pending value recorded as missed by 21.2×.** This is the same shape
as [DR-029](DR-029-temp-accuracy-mismatch-not-met.md)'s temperature-accuracy
rows: target not relaxed, miss recorded honestly, decision routed through
this record rather than silently absorbed.

**The project's "tiny area rides along on any shuttle seat" framing
(DR-001's own cited rationale for the 3.3 V supply-flavor choice, and this
project's general self-description) is now measurably not representative of
the current implementation's footprint.** 1.059 mm² is not "tiny" by the
standard that framing implies. This does not retract
[DR-001](DR-001-supply-flavor.md)'s decision itself, which turned on a
*relative* comparison (3.3 V core devices vs. 5 V-flavor thick-oxide devices
being "meaningfully larger... for the same function"), not an absolute area
ceiling — the relative comparison stands regardless of the absolute number.
But wherever the "tiny"/"rides along" framing appears as if it were an
established fact about the built block (rather than a wave-1 aspiration),
it needs a caveat pointing at this record; `README.md`'s layout-status
section is updated by #211 alongside this record for that reason.

**The regenerable-evidence property this record depends on is now permanent
infrastructure, not a one-off measurement.** `layout/run_checks.sh`'s new
step 4 (`klt stats`) runs for every cell on every invocation, gated by the
same GDS-hash drift check as the DRC/extraction/LVS reports
(`lvs_reference.GDS_HASH_FIELDS["stats.json"]`), so a future layout
revision's `area` figure is regenerated, not hand-edited, the next time this
script runs — see `layout/README.md` § `temp_por_top`, "Footprint / area".

**Which number a future area-reduction effort should target is not decided
here.** The 0.342 mm² sub-cell-sum vs. 1.059 mm² footprint gap is attributed
(not root-caused per line item) to `TOP_PLACEMENT`'s inter-instance spacing,
the guard-ring width and the rail width — the likely first levers if a
future revision is chartered to reduce area, since they cost no sub-cell
redesign — but no such effort is proposed, scoped or authorized by this
record.

**`spec/target-spec.md#area`'s Evidence column and §8 Open TBD register are
updated to point at this record and #211** in place of the closed #17, per
this table's own convention for a filled TBD row (e.g.
`~~[temp-vt-transfer]~~ ... filled by #9`).
